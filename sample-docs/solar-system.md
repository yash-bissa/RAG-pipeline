# Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all objects that orbit it.

## The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of the Solar System. It contains most of the system's mass and provides the energy that drives Earth's climate.

## The Planets

There are eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

Venus is the hottest planet because its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere creates an extreme greenhouse effect. Although Mercury is closer to the Sun, Venus has a much higher surface temperature.

## Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet with life. It has liquid water on its surface and an atmosphere rich in nitrogen and oxygen.
